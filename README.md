## About

Bloom & Burn is a complete one-page daily fitness system:

- **Morning plan** — 7-day at-home workouts (MIZI videos) with per-day yoga warm-ups and targeted add-ons (thigh, arm, stomach, upper body, lower body & hip, full body)
- **Evening plan** — 7-day strength & cardio plan with a daily 10-minute warm-up and weight-loss tips
- **Workout calendars** — tap-to-mark days, with automatic missed-day marking
- **Water tracker** — daily countdown (2L to 3L) with a hidden hourly log
- **Sleep Cycle** — 9 PM to 5 AM tracker with a hidden sleep log
- **Menstrual Cycle** — calendar with cycle-phase estimates and rotating phase quotes
- **Journal & Notes** — a daily journal page plus manifestation notes and affirmations
- **High Energy** — auto-rotating motivational quotes, and an animated companion that shares spoken motivation
